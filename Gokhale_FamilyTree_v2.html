The Gokhales of Shishila

A Message to the Family

While historical records leave the exact origins and migration of the Chitpavan community to Dakshina Kannada shrouded in time, much of our own ancestry has also remained unwritten, passed down primarily through memory rather than documented lineage. Born out of deep curiosity and a heartfelt desire to rediscover our roots, this family tree is an endeavor to trace the generations that came before us. Over the years, changing circumstances led the joint family to branch into nuclear homes, scattering our kin across different regions. This record stands as a humble effort to bridge those distances and reunite the widely dispersed families of the Shishila Gokhales under one shared legacy.

To make this historical tapestry truly complete, an initiative is also underway to compile photographs of our family members into a commemorative album, and your support in sharing these memories would be invaluable. As with any effort to piece together the past, there may be omissions or inaccuracies; I warmly welcome your insights, corrections, and additions to help enrich this collective chronicle.

With warm regards and gratitude,

Your own family member,
Manohara M. Gokhale
